# TSUKUMO99 / DiŹ visual system

## Core world

An unofficial fan project staged with the confidence of a real Japanese artist site. The visual language combines nightclub lighting, editorial information systems, collectible ephemera, dice pips, and decisive oversized typography.

## Tokens

- Ink `#170006`, wine `#3a0014`, crimson `#8d002c`
- Hot pink `#ec0050`, pink `#ff86bd`
- Paper `#fff4f7`, muted `#e4afbf`
- Display: self-hosted Oswald
- Utility/data: self-hosted Space Mono
- CJK body: system Noto Sans SC / JP fallbacks

## Page grammar

- A persistent top identity and route navigation joins Top, News, Profile, and Shop.
- Key visuals stay Latin/stylized; interface content follows CN / JP / EN.
- Profile is an immersive full-viewport reveal: typographic ribbons, switch, dice roll, member result.
- News is a restrained editorial ledger: category filters, dated rows, expandable detail.
- Shop is a collectible grid: select, enter one name, generate and download a fan-made PNG.
- Ticket export and draggable cheer behavior remain isolated and unchanged.

## Motion

- Micro interactions: 0.2–0.35s.
- Structural transitions: 0.5–0.8s with strong ease-out.
- Atmospheric typography may drift continuously.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` removes non-essential animation.

## Content boundaries

- Lead identity is TSUKUMO99 + DiŹ; never use the retired legacy band name.
- Five member names and bios remain neutral placeholders until supplied.
- No payment, address, email, or real commerce.
- Every generated artifact is marked unofficial / fan-made.
